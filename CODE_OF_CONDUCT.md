# Code of Conduct

ForgeMind AI follows a simple standard: collaborate respectfully, review ideas on technical merit, and do not harass or discriminate against contributors.

Expected behavior includes constructive feedback, clear communication, respect for privacy, and responsible handling of industrial safety and security topics. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, publishing private information, deliberate security abuse, or misrepresenting model capabilities.

Concerns may be reported privately to the maintainer through [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mohamad-ktich) or `ktichmohamad@gmail.com`.
